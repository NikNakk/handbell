Install & help

iPhone: Open this site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen (enable Open as Web App if offered).

Android: Open in Chrome, open the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

Motion requires HTTPS and permission. Keep the app open and hold your phone securely. If sound is muted, check media volume and silent mode. The app works offline after the first successful online load. Motion is processed only on your device.
